A LITTLE PASSWORD KNOW-HOW
01

Length beats complexity

Long passwords take far longer to guess. Aim for at least 12 characters; 16 or more is even better.

02

Make every one unique

Reusing passwords means one data breach can put multiple accounts at risk. A password manager can help.

03

Entropy, in a nutshell

Entropy estimates how unpredictable a password is. Randomness and length both make guessing harder.
